## Meaning

eIDAS is the EU framework for electronic identification and trust services. eIDAS 2.0 extends this context with the European Digital Identity framework and the EUDI Wallet ecosystem.

For this project, eIDAS is the legal background, while the ARF is the architecture and ecosystem explanation.
